Support

Feed Analytics for LinkedIn

Before reporting a problem

  1. Confirm the extension is enabled in edge://extensions.
  2. Open the LinkedIn home feed and refresh the page.
  3. Scroll through several feed posts to allow the analytics panel to populate.
  4. If LinkedIn recently changed its page layout, classification may temporarily require an extension update.

Support requests

For bugs, compatibility issues, privacy questions, or other support requests, use the public GitHub repository for policies/support:

github.com/zerobline/policies/issues

Please do not include passwords, private messages, confidential information, or screenshots containing information you do not want to make public.

Feed Analytics for LinkedIn is an independent browser extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by LinkedIn Corporation or Microsoft.
