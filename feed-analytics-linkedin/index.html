Feed Analytics for LinkedIn

A Microsoft Edge extension that helps users understand what is filling the LinkedIn feed currently displayed in their browser.

What it does

The extension categorizes visible feed items, shows local source counts, allows feed-category filtering, and can generate a CSV export on demand.

Processing occurs locally in Microsoft Edge. The extension does not send LinkedIn feed data or analytics results to a developer server.

Feed Analytics for LinkedIn is an independent browser extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by LinkedIn Corporation or Microsoft.
