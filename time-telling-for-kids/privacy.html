Privacy Policy — Time Telling for Kids

Effective date: 28 September 2026

Summary: Time Telling for Kids does not require an account, does not contain advertising or analytics, and does not send a child's learning progress or personal information to Zerobline servers.

1. Information we collect

Time Telling for Kids does not ask users to provide a name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

The app does not include a Zerobline account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or developer-operated backend for collecting user data.

2. Learning progress and settings

Practice results, points, level progress, and app settings are stored locally on the user's device. This information is used only to provide the app's progress features and is not transmitted to Zerobline.

Removing the app or clearing its app data may delete this locally stored information.

3. Audio and text-to-speech

The app can use the Android device's text-to-speech functionality to read prompts and answers aloud. The app passes the text to the text-to-speech service available on the device. Zerobline does not receive the spoken text, audio, or speech output.

The handling of text by a device's text-to-speech provider may be subject to that provider's own privacy practices and device settings.

4. Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app does not contain third-party advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics functionality.

5. Children's privacy

The app is designed as an educational tool for children. It is designed to operate without requiring children to submit personal information to Zerobline.

6. Data sharing and sale

Zerobline does not sell personal information collected through this app. Because the app does not transmit user progress or personal information to Zerobline, Zerobline does not share such data with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Security

The app minimizes privacy risk by keeping learning progress on the device and by not operating a user-account or analytics backend.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this privacy policy will be updated before or alongside the relevant app update.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be submitted through the project's public GitHub presence at github.com/zerobline.

This policy applies specifically to the Android app Time Telling for Kids, package name com.zerobline.timetellingforkids.
